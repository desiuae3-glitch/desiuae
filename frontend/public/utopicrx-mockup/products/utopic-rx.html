Frequently asked questions

Can I still open the door with a key?

Yes — anytime, including emergencies. Keep a spare key as backup.

Will installation damage my door?

No extra holes. Mounts on the cylinder with screws or adhesive only.

Does it work with multi-point locks?

Yes. Auto-calibrates the number of turns during first-time setup.

Do I need internet?

No for daily use — BLE app control works offline. Smart Bridge adds remote access.

How long does install take?

Typically 1–5 minutes with the included guide and accessories.

Can I move it when I leave?

Yes. Unscrew and reinstall on your next door — ideal for tenants.
