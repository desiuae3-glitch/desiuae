Frequently asked questions

Tap any question below to expand the full answer. Every response is written for UAE renters and homeowners who want practical facts before they order.

Will my deposit be at risk?
No. Utopic R mounts on your existing euro-profile cylinder without drilling or altering the lock body. When you move out, unscrew the unit and the door looks exactly as before. There is no permanent modification landlords can cite. Many Dubai and Sharjah renters choose it because installation is fully reversible. Photograph your door before and after install, keep any removed thumbturn parts, and share our renter guide with your landlord.
Do I need landlord permission?
In the UAE, tenants typically need landlord consent before changing locks or fittings. Utopic R is a reversible add-on, not a replacement lock, which many landlords accept once they see no holes are drilled. Send photos with our one-page approval template. Your physical key still works and the lock removes cleanly at end of tenancy. If your lease bans hardware changes, get written permission first via WhatsApp.
What if the battery dies?
A dead battery never traps you inside or out. Your physical key always works regardless of charge. The DESi Smart app alerts at 20% and 10%, and battery level is always visible. Expect up to 3 months on a full charge via Micro USB depending on use. Plug a power bank into the Micro USB port for temporary charging. Enable energy-saving mode before long trips to extend runtime.
Can it be hacked?
BLE uses AES-128 encryption and proximity authentication via the DESi Smart app. The base lock has no always-on internet; remote access needs the optional WiFi Bridge with TLS. Signed over-the-air firmware updates ship regularly. No lock is unhackable, but Utopic R follows standard smart-lock security. Keep the app updated, lock your phone, and revoke guest access when no longer needed.
Does it work without WiFi?
Yes for daily unlocking. Bluetooth BLE 5.0 links your phone within a few metres. Create offline one-time or time-limited e-keys without internet. Auto-lock, logs, and user management work fully offline. WiFi is only needed for remote access, Alexa, Google Home, Siri, and Home Assistant. Many UAE renters skip the Bridge and unlock by phone or keypad. Your physical key always works.
Can I take it when I move?
Yes. Unmount four screws or peel 3M pads, remove the thumbturn adapter if fitted, and pack the lock for your next place. Reinstalling on a new euro-profile door takes three to five minutes. Nothing permanent remains, so deposit disputes over door hardware are unlikely. Keep the original box. Multi-point locks auto-calibrate turn count on the new door during setup. Read our renter overview.
Does it work on my door (euro-cylinder compatibility)?
Nearly every UAE apartment and villa uses a euro-profile oval cylinder. Utopic R clamps on with no cylinder swap. You need two millimetres gap between cylinder tail and door surface, or use the adhesive mount. Thumbturn cylinders need the supplied adapter. Multi-point locks are supported with auto turn calibration. Send a door photo on WhatsApp and we confirm fit within hours.
How long does installation take?
Most customers finish in three to five minutes first try. Four screws on the cylinder tail, direction calibration in the app, and a test cycle. No drilling, electrician, or locksmith required. Free professional install is available in Dubai and Sharjah. Video guide and quick-start card ship in the box. Thumbturn conversions add one step but stay under ten minutes.
Is there a UAE warranty?
Yes. Every Utopic R from utopicrx.com includes a two-year warranty on lock body, electronics, motor, and rechargeable battery. We are the authorized UAE distributor with local RMA collection across all Emirates via WhatsApp or support@utopicrx.com. Misuse, unauthorised repair, and water damage beyond rated IP are excluded. Battery capacity loss above thirty percent is covered. Full terms on our Returns & Warranty page.
What’s the return policy?
Return unused, unopened, unpaired units within seven days for change of mind, minus AED 100 restocking fee with original packaging. Installed, paired, or biometric-enrolled products are not eligible for change-of-mind returns but stay under two-year warranty. Defects reported within seven days get free collection and full refund or replacement. See our Returns & Warranty policy for RMA steps and refund timelines.
