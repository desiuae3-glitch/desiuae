Compatibility

Euro-cylinder compatibility — no modification needed

Nearly every apartment and villa door in the UAE uses a euro-profile (oval) cylinder. If your key turns a round or oval lock barrel, the Utopic R fits. The lock clamps onto the inside thumbturn — the part you twist to unlock from indoors — and drives it electronically.

You keep your existing keys. Your landlord’s master key still works. Emergency access from outside is never blocked, thanks to patented key-through technology that lets you use a physical key even when another key is inserted inside. There is no cylinder replacement, no key cutting, and no visible change to the exterior of the door.

Not sure if your door qualifies? Send us a photo on WhatsApp and we will confirm compatibility within hours. Minimum requirement: a 2 mm gap between the cylinder face and the door surface.
