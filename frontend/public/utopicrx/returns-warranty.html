Section 02

Damaged or defective products

If your product arrives damaged, defective, or develops a manufacturing fault within 7 days of delivery, we will arrange free collection and offer a full refund or free replacement at no cost to you.

How to raise a claim

  • Email support@utopicrx.com or WhatsApp us within 7 days of delivery.
  • Include your order number, photos and a short video of the defect, and a brief description.
  • We will issue an RMA number within 1 business day and arrange free courier collection.

After day 7, manufacturing defects are covered under our 2-year manufacturer's warranty — see Section 06.

Section 03

Change of mind

You may return an unused product within 7 calendar days of delivery, subject to the conditions below.

Eligibility

  • Not installed on a door, drawer, or any fixture.
  • Not paired to the DESi Smart app or any user account.
  • Free of enrolled fingerprints, PIN codes, RFID cards, or face data.
  • In original packaging with all accessories, manuals, screws, and seals intact.
  • Free from scratches, drilling marks, adhesive residue, or signs of fitting.

Restocking fee

  • Restocking fee — AED 100 (inspection, factory reset, repackaging)

Outbound delivery charges paid at purchase are non-refundable.

Section 04

Non-returnable items

  • Products installed, paired, or with enrolled biometrics.
  • Custom-configured, engraved, or special-order units.
  • Products with missing accessories, manuals, or original packaging.
  • Products damaged by improper installation, misuse, water ingress, or unauthorised modification.
  • Project, trade, or B2B orders governed by the issued PO.

Section 06

Warranty coverage

All DESi smart locks and accessories sold by us are covered by a 2-year manufacturer's warranty from the date of delivery. The warranty covers manufacturing defects and component failures under normal residential use.

Coverage by component

  • Lock body, cylinder, electronics, fingerprint sensor, keypad, and face reader — 24 months.
  • Built-in rechargeable battery — 24 months against manufacturing defect or capacity loss exceeding 30%.
  • Alkaline batteries supplied with wireless readers — not warranted (first-use only).
  • App, cloud connectivity, and OTA updates — provided as available.

Not covered

  • Improper installation, misuse, or unauthorised repair.
  • Water ingress beyond the product's rated IP class.
  • Lightning, power surges, or extreme environmental conditions.
  • Cosmetic wear from normal use.
  • Third-party hardware, software, or network issues.
  • Removed, altered, or illegible serial numbers.

To raise a warranty claim, email support@utopicrx.com with your order number, fault description, and photos or video.

Section 07

Damage on delivery

Inspect outer packaging at delivery. If the carton is visibly damaged, refuse delivery or accept with a written note to the courier. Notify us within 24 hours with photos of the carton and product.

Section 08

Refunds

Refunds go to the original payment method. Card refunds typically appear within 7–14 working days. Bank transfers within 5 working days. Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer. We do not issue cash refunds.
