Common questions

Asked by UAE buyers.

Will the adhesive damage my door?

No. The 3M VHB tape shipped with every lock is residue-free on treated wood, metal, and uPVC. Tested across standard UAE villa and apartment doors — it removes cleanly with a hair-dryer.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Face, fingerprint, PIN, and card all work fully offline. Wi-Fi is only needed for app-based remote unlocking and e-key sharing.

What if the battery dies?

Low-battery warnings appear 30 days in advance via the app. In an emergency, plug any USB-C power bank into the underside port, or use the included mechanical override key.

Can I return it if it doesn't fit my door?

Yes — 7-day return on unused locks. Better still, book the free survey first — we confirm compatibility before you order.

Is the face recognition safe?

Yes. Face data is encrypted with AES-256 GCM (bank-grade) and stored locally on the lock — never on a server. Anti-spoofing defeats photo and video attacks.
